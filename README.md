# Rocco's Rescue Road

a city traffic game for Rocco, age 3. a big road in front of a skyline and a hospital, jammed with eight colorful cars, trucks, vans, and a bus.

- **Rescue buttons:** three giant buttons - FIRE TRUCK, POLICE CAR, and AMBULANCE. tap one and its siren sounds (each vehicle has its own: fire wail, police yelp, ambulance two-tone), its lights flash, every traffic car pulls over to the sides of the road, and the rescue vehicle drives straight through with a "WEE-WOO!" bubble
- **Way to go:** after a rescue, traffic rolls back into the jam and Rocco earns a rescue star, with a star burst and the "WAY TO GO, ROCCO!" banner
- **Tap the traffic:** every traffic car honks and hops when tapped, with a "Beep beep!" bubble. plus "Everybody honk!" and "New traffic" (reshuffles the cars) buttons
- **More to tap:** the sun and clouds are tappable, and there's a sound on/off button for grown-ups
- **Tech:** one self-contained `index.html` - city, vehicles, and every sound drawn and synthesized in code (Web Audio), no assets
- no fail states, no timers, no reading required

Fourth in the series: [Rocco's Garage](https://github.com/roccozzie/roccos-garage) (tire swapping), [Wee Woo](https://github.com/roccozzie/wee-woo) (Ozzie's emergency vehicles), and [Captain Rocco](https://github.com/roccozzie/captain-rocco) (commercial airliner).
